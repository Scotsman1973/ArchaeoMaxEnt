about=ArchaeoMaxEnt models the probability of archaeological site locations with MaxEnt. It works from common geospatial formats (GeoTIFF elevation DEM, Shapefile, GeoPackage), derives slope and aspect from the DEM, and lets the researcher weight each environmental class before modelling. Sensible defaults are suggested and can be changed, with every step logged, to increase transparency and support open science. Modelling runs in a separate, version-locked runtime (Python with elapid, geopandas, rasterio and scikit-learn at fixed versions) that is installed once from a single archive per platform, by download or offline from a file. Nothing is installed into QGIS's own Python, and the same plugin works on Windows, macOS and Linux with QGIS 3.22 or later, including QGIS 4.
